## How to sign

On your first pull request, the CLA bot posts a comment with a link to
this document. Reply with the sentence it gives:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub account name, the date and the pull request number are then
recorded in `signatures/cla.json` on the `cla-signatures` branch of this
repository.
